# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want everyone to take part in this project without harassment, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Do:

- Be kind and patient.
- Respect opinions and experiences that are different from yours.
- Give and accept feedback in a constructive way.
- Say when you are wrong, and learn from it.

Do not:

- Use sexual language or images, or make sexual advances.
- Troll, insult, or attack people.
- Harass anyone, in public or in private.
- Publish private information of other people without their permission.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere a person
represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report bad behavior to the maintainer through the contact options on
<https://github.com/codeFi>. All reports are looked at and kept private.

The maintainer can remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions
that do not follow this code, and can block a person for a time or permanently.
