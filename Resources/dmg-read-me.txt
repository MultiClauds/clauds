Clauds
===============

INSTALL

1. Drag "Clauds" to the "Applications" folder.
2. Open "Clauds" from Applications.


macOS SAYS IT CANNOT OPEN THE APP. THIS IS NORMAL.

This app is not notarized. "Notarized" means that the developer paid Apple
for a developer account and Apple scanned the app. This is a free project
and has no such account. So macOS blocks the first start.

To permit the app, one time:

1. Try to open the app. A warning shows. Click "Done". Do NOT click
   "Move to Trash".
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Go down to "Security". Click "Open Anyway" next to "Clauds".
4. Click "Open Anyway" again, and type your Mac password.

Or use Terminal, one time:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Clauds.app"

After that, the app opens as usual.

You can also build the app from the source code. Then macOS shows no
warning. See the project page.
